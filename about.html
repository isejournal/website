About the journal

A venue for student economics held to a standard above coursework, run by students, refereed by academics.

What we publish

Long, refereed, evidence-based economic writing by students: pieces of 4,000 to 6,000 words with a defensible empirical or analytical basis, answering a question the author framed. Work that began as coursework or a dissertation is eligible; work published or under consideration elsewhere is not.

Causal language only where identification supports it. Where an argument rests on data and code, both are published with the piece.

Three differences

Real review

Two external referees on every paper that passes the desk, working to a fixed report form. No decision after review rests on one report.

A standard above coursework

Papers are longer and more self-directed than assessed work, and are judged on identification, interpretation and presentation, not on effort.

Scale through partnership

Editors, referees and advisers come from many universities. The journal is independent of any one of them.

How review works

  1. Screeninganonymised by Operations within five working days of receipt
  2. Desk decisionSection Head, within two weeks; desk rejections co-signed by the cluster Deputy
  3. Two refereesreports due 21 days after acceptance
  4. RecommendationSection Head, second-read by the cluster Deputy
  5. Final decisionChief Editor, within eight weeks of receipt
  6. Revisionminor 14 days, major 28; one round of major revision
  7. Data and code checkevery accepted empirical piece is reproduced before production
  8. Productiontypeset and published in the next issue whose cut-off it meets

Authors have a right of appeal on procedural error, demonstrable misreading, or undeclared conflict. See Policies.

Seven sections

Grouped in three clusters, each led by a Deputy Editor.

  • Applied Micro, Development and Labour
  • Political Economy and Public Policy
  • Macroeconomics
  • Finance and Financial Markets
  • Econometrics and Data Science, with the methods and data desk
  • Trade, Law and Institutions
  • Energy, Environment and Digital Infrastructure

How we are run

The journal is run by its members under a written constitution adopted by its founding Board. The Board of editors and leads decides policy and reserved matters; the Executive runs the journal week to week; three lines, editorial, administrative and external, do the work. No university, department, society, funder, sponsor or adviser has any right of review or veto over editorial content.

Questions

Who can submit?

Undergraduate students at any university, and graduates within twelve months for work done as a student. Every co-author must be eligible.

Is review anonymous?

In both directions. Operations holds author identities; editors and referees work from a paper ID.

May I use AI tools?

Language editing and coding help, with disclosure. Text, analysis or results generated by a tool and presented as your own are not accepted.

How long does it take?

A decision within eight weeks of receipt.

Is there a fee?

To be confirmed before submissions open.
