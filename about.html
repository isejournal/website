About the journal

A refereed journal of undergraduate economics, run by students, with external referees from academia and practice.

ISEJ is the first globally-run triple-blind peer-reviewed journal for undergraduate economics, and aims to provide an initial entrypoint into the world of economics research to undergraduates around the world. Authors which pass the desk-decision stage receive their referee reports regardless of the final editorial decision.

Scope

Long-form, evidence-based economic writing by undergraduate students. We accept papers of 4,000 to 6,000 words with a defensible empirical or analytical basis, answering a question the author framed. Work that began as coursework or a dissertation is eligible; work published or under consideration elsewhere is not.

Causal language should be used only where identification supports it. Where an argument rests on data and code, both are published with the paper.

Governance

The journal is run by its members under a written constitution adopted by its founding Board. The Board decides policy and reserved matters. An Executive runs the journal week to week through three lines: editorial, administrative, and external. No university, department, society, funder, sponsor, or adviser has any right of review or veto over editorial content.

Editorial board and team

Review

Two external referees read every paper that passes the desk, working to a fixed report form. No decision after review rests on one report. Review is triple-blind; see Policies.

Given that ISEJ is a journal for undergraduate research, the core editorial team primarily consists of undergraduates as well. Their economic judgement is supplemented by that of the referees for papers which make it past the desk.

  1. 1ScreeningAnonymised by Operations within five working days of receipt
  2. 2Desk decisionSection Head, within two weeks; a desk rejection is co-signed by the cluster Deputy
  3. 3Two refereesReports due 21 days after the referee accepts
  4. 4RecommendationSection Head, second read by the cluster Deputy
  5. 5DecisionEditor in Chief, with a target of eight weeks from receipt
  6. 6Revision14 days minor, 28 days major; one round of major revision
  7. 7Reproduction checkEvery accepted empirical paper is reproduced before production
  8. 8PublicationIn the next issue whose cut-off the paper meets

Authors may appeal a rejection on procedural error, a demonstrable misreading, or an undeclared conflict. See Policies.

Sections

Seven sections in three clusters, each cluster led by a Deputy Editor.

  • Applied Micro, Development, and Labour
  • Political Economy and Public Policy
  • Macroeconomics
  • Finance and Financial Markets
  • Econometrics and Data Sciencewith the methods and data desk, which serves every section
  • Trade, Law, and Institutions
  • Energy, Environment, and Digital Infrastructure

Questions

Who can submit?

Undergraduate students in any year and university as well as graduates within twelve months of graduation for work done as a student. Every co-author must be eligible.

May I use AI tools?

Language editing and coding help may be used with full disclosure. Text, analysis, or results generated by a tool and presented as your own work will not be accepted.

How long does a decision take?

We aim to decide within eight weeks of receipt.
