Call for papers

Long, refereed work by students. Submissions are handled as they arrive, each on its own clock.

Eligibility

Undergraduate students registered at any university, and graduates within twelve months of graduation for work done while a student. Every co-author must be eligible. Work that began as coursework or a dissertation is welcome; work published or under consideration elsewhere is not.

Length and standard

4,000 to 6,000 words, excluding references, tables, figures and appendices. The bar is a strong undergraduate paper: a real question, appropriate data or theory, a stated identification strategy and its limits, results interpreted with care.

The most common reasons papers are rejected after review:

  • causal claims the design cannot support;
  • results described but not interpreted;
  • a question the paper leaves unanswered.

What happens next

  1. Day 5screened and anonymised; you receive a paper ID and an expected decision date
  2. Week 2desk decision by the Section Head
  3. Week 3two referees secured
  4. Week 6reports received
  5. Week 8decision letter: accept, minor revision, major revision, or reject
  6. Revision14 days minor, 28 days major; one round of major revision
  7. Acceptancedata and code check and line edit, then the next issue

What to send

  • the manuscript, with no author-identifying information;
  • a separate title page: authors, affiliations, contact details, acknowledgements;
  • a cover note of up to 200 words: the question, the contribution, coursework or dissertation origin, any prior circulation;
  • a data and code declaration;
  • an AI-use disclosure.

Author guidelines

To follow before submissions open.

AI-use disclosure

Language editing and coding assistance are acceptable with disclosure. Text, analysis or results generated by an AI tool and presented as the author’s own are not. See Policies.

Data and code

A piece whose argument rests on data analysis is published with the data and code that produce its tables and figures, deposited with a persistent identifier. See Policies.
