Map of the site

Every line on this site is a contour of one function. Each page is drawn from a different place on it: a saddle, a basin, a rise. Choose a place.

Nine numbered places on one contour field, one for each page; each marker is a link to its page. Moving the pointer over the map, tapping open ground, or moving keyboard focus to a marker shows the position, the height of the field and the shape of the ground at that place in the panel at the foot of the map, and draws the contour line at that height.
