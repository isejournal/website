Refereed economics, written by students

Long, evidence-based papers by undergraduates, read by two external referees. Call for papers

Dates

Submissions are handled as they arrive, each on its own clock, with a decision within eight weeks of receipt. Two issues a year.

Review

Every paper that passes the editors’ desk goes to two external referees. Editors decide on the evidence of the reports, without knowing who the author is, and authors are never told who refereed them.

How review works

Scope

Papers of 4,000 to 6,000 words with a defensible empirical or analytical basis, in seven sections across economics: applied micro, development and labour; political economy and public policy; macroeconomics; finance; econometrics and data science; trade, law and institutions; energy, environment and digital infrastructure.
