Academic Advisory Board

Guidance on standards and direction, about an hour a term.

The role

Advisers give guidance on the journal’s standards and on topics worth commissioning, and join one call a term. They take no part in individual submissions, do no day-to-day work, carry no responsibility for what is published and hold no vote. The role is unpaid. An adviser may referee in a personal capacity.

Advisers are listed here with their consent, with affiliation given for identification only, and confirm the listing each year.

Write to isej.org@gmail.com if you would like to be considered.
