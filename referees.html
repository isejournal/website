Referees

Faculty, doctoral students, early-career researchers, and practitioners are all welcome. Your report is the main evidence for the editors’ decision.

The report

A report is designed to take about an hour, on a fixed form of eight short sections: summary, question and contribution, data and identification, interpretation, presentation, ratings, recommendation, and confidential comments to the editors. If it regularly takes more than ninety minutes, we will shorten the form. You are asked for at most two reports a term, each due 21 days after you accept. Being in the pool does not oblige you to accept any particular invitation.

Referees comment on identification, economic reasoning, interpretation, and presentation. Editorial and eligibility decisions, revisions, and all correspondence with authors stay with the editorial team.

Anonymity

Review is triple-blind; see Policies. Referees are credited in an annual acknowledgement unless they opt out, and will never be identified to authors.

Joining the pool

If the form does not load, write to operations@isejournal.org with your institution, career stage, fields, and the methods you can assess.
