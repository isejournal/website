What we ask

Comment on identification, economic reasoning, interpretation of results and presentation, on a fixed form of eight short sections. Being in the pool does not oblige you to accept any particular invitation.

What referees do not do

Editorial or eligibility decisions, revisions, or correspondence with authors. Those stay with the student editorial team.

What you get

Early experience of refereeing; an annual acknowledgement unless you opt out; you are never identified to authors.
