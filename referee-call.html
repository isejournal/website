Call for Referees

The International Student Economics Journal (ISEJ) is recruiting external referees for undergraduate economics research.

View or download this call as a PDF (2 pages, 104 KB)

About ISEJ

ISEJ is a peer-reviewed economics journal run by students from 23 universities worldwide, including Oxford, Princeton, LSE, Cambridge, Stanford, Berkeley, NUS, and Yale.

Our Senior Advisory Board includes two Yrjö Jahnsson Award recipients, former Presidents of the American Economic Association, Econometric Society, and American Finance Association, recipients of the Frisch Medal and BBVA Foundation Frontiers of Knowledge Award, a former editor of the American Economic Review, a Fellow of the British Academy, a former Director of the Institute for Fiscal Studies, and senior economists affiliated with the European Central Bank and World Bank.

We publish long-form undergraduate economics research of 4,000–6,000 words, held to a standard above ordinary coursework. Papers that pass initial editorial screening are reviewed by two external referees, matched by field and methodological expertise.

Who we are looking for

PhD/DPhil and MPhil students, postdoctoral researchers, teaching fellows, college tutors, junior faculty, or other early-career economists across economics and related fields.

Our areas include applied microeconomics; development and labour; political economy and public policy; macroeconomics; finance and financial markets; econometrics and data science; trade, law and institutions; and energy, environmental and digital economics.

What refereeing involves

  • Approximately 60 minutes per report
  • No more than two papers per academic term
  • No obligation to accept any particular invitation
  • A structured referee form covering the paper’s contribution, evidence and identification or analytical basis, interpretation, and presentation
  • Anonymised review in both directions

Refereeing is currently unpaid, although we are exploring funding to support referee compensation in the future.
