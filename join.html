Entry roles are the route to the senior ones. Senior roles are filled from within.

Entry roles

  • Junior Editor: referee handling, revisions, line edits; the pool for future Section Heads and Deputies
  • Operations Coordinator: screening, anonymisation, records, author correspondence
  • Production assistant: typesetting support before each issue
  • Campus representative: your department’s link to the journal, held alongside another role

Doctoral students and early-career researchers are welcome in the referee pool. All roles are unpaid.
