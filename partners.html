Departments, societies, and sponsors

We have two ways to associate with the journal. Neither gives any right over what it publishes.

Departments and societies

There are four ways to help us, none of which require funding or editorial responsibility. These are circulating the calls for papers and referees, introduction to colleagues who might referee, provision of academics for the Advisory Board, and recognition in whichever forms your department approves.

Sponsors and funders

We require funding to pay for the domain, hosting, and systems now, and later we may pay referee honoraria. Therefore, we would be grateful for sponsorship, grants, and in-kind support.

Independence

Editorial integrity is one of our strongest commitments and we therefore view independence as vital to our operations. Nobody on the sponsorship side takes part in referee selection or any editorial decision. Editors are not made aware of any sponsor’s individual impact and no agreement may grant a sponsor any right over editorial content.

Write to outreach@isejournal.org, if you are interested in providing support detailing what you are willing to offer. All support is greatly appreciated.
