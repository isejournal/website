Departments, societies, sponsors and funders

Work with the journal

Two ways to associate with the journal. Neither gives any right over what it publishes.

Departments and societies

None of this requires funding or editorial responsibility from a department. Four asks, in order of ease:

  • one email a term circulating the calls for papers and referees
  • introductions to colleagues who might referee
  • one or two academics for the Advisory Board
  • recognition in whatever form the department approves

Anything public naming a department is sent to it for approval first. No university logo is used. Downloads of the calls, as circulars to forward unedited, to follow.

Sponsors and funders

Money pays for the domain, hosting and systems now, and may pay referee honoraria later. Sponsorship, grants and in-kind support are all welcome.

The firewall

Nobody on the sponsorship side takes part in referee selection or any editorial decision. Editors are not told which sponsor funds what. No sponsor sits on any prize jury. No agreement may grant a sponsor any right over editorial content.
